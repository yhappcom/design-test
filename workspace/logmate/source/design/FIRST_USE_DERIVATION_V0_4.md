# LogMate First-Use Derivation from HOME-B v0.4

Status: PORTFOLIO RENDER CONTRACT / NOT LOCKED
Date: 2026-09-19

## Rule
Welcome / Previous Total / Empty Home must inherit HOME-B v0.4 visual language. They may reduce density but may not invent a new palette, card system, icon system, typography hierarchy or marketing language.

## Welcome
Required:
- `LogMate`
- `Pilot Logbook`
- primary `Start a new logbook`
- secondary `Sign in`

Forbidden:
- feature tour;
- fake flight data;
- aviation imagery;
- marketing slogan;
- `Access anywhere`;
- `Private and secure` or similar unsupported trust superlative;
- ads;
- bottom navigation.

Composition:
- lowest-density expression of HOME-B system;
- typography and whitespace carry identity;
- one primary action boundary, one secondary text action;
- no card/panel.

## Previous Total — decision
Required:
- `Previous Total`
- concise explanation of carried-forward totals before first LogMate record;
- explicit `Start from zero`;
- explicit `Carry forward totals`;
- Continue disabled until a choice is made.

Interaction:
- rows/selection use authored control geometry, not giant radio cards;
- selected state survives grayscale.

## Previous Total — carry-forward
Required:
- sparse semantic totals only;
- one H+MM editor per duration semantic;
- blank != zero;
- `Add previous total` for additional approved semantics;
- full semantic labels.

Illustrative values for composition only:
- Block 2,350+25
- Night 412+10
- Instrument Flight Time 1,125+30

Do not render generic Total Flight Time / Total Flights / Total Landings as a replacement for the semantic baseline model.

## Empty Home
Rule:
Empty Home is HOME-B with record-dependent regions collapsed/empty, not a separately branded empty-state screen.

Required:
- same Home header/settings shell;
- Add Flight primary;
- View Logbook remains available where product flow permits;
- Search may remain present or become inactive based on final product behavior;
- explicit `No flights yet.` state;
- Import entry remains optional and secondary;
- Previous Total state/recovery access where appropriate.

Forbidden:
- fake Recent/Activity/Totals;
- zero-filled KPI dashboard;
- decorative illustration;
- motivational slogan;
- ads.

## First Populated Home
Not part of this immediate render unless needed for continuity.
It must be HOME-B with the first real record, remain ad-free, and avoid celebratory marketing copy.
