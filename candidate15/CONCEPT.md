## Concept
Signal Ledger treats Home as a precise operational ledger rather than a dashboard. It uses a warm paper-neutral light surface / graphite dark surface, near-black typography, saturated vermilion functional accent, thin explicit ledger rules, compact square controls, and restrained density. No pastel atmosphere, rounded-card stack, aviation decoration, glass, metallic, or cockpit simulation.
